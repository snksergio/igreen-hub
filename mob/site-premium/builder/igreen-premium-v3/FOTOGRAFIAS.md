# Variação fotográfica do ecossistema

Cinco imagens conceituais geradas com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, seguindo a mesma direção visual da hero. Esta galeria foi duplicada abaixo da seção de maquetes no mesmo `dist/index.html`, com seleções independentes.

## Prompts finais

### energia

Use case: ads-marketing. One photograph in a unified five-image premium electric-mobility campaign for iGreen MOB. Reference image role: art direction for emerald green automotive paint, urban architecture, realistic lighting and materials. Make a NEW photographic composition, not a duplicate. A real full-scale urban world, absolutely NOT an isometric miniature, not a diorama, not an icon, no plinth. Identical art direction across the series: refined contemporary charcoal concrete and smoked glass architecture, deep emerald green and subtle precise neon-green architectural light accents, neutral white practical lights, controlled nighttime/blue-hour exposure, black shadows with readable detail, clean reflective surfaces, realistic restrained light bloom. Premium automotive advertising photography, 35mm lens feel, physically plausible proportions, subtle film grain, natural high-detail materials, no exaggerated CGI gloss. Landscape 4:3 framing, main subject centered in the middle 70% with some breathing room, usable dark space along the bottom for an external caption. No baked-in words, numbers, typography, watermark, brand badges, floating icons, people or UI overlays. Avoid yellow/gold lighting, purple/blue color casts and daylight. Subject: ENERGY. A full-scale modern charcoal urban house at blue hour, seen from street level three-quarter view. A flat roof with a realistic black solar panel array, neutral softly lit living-room glass, a discreet emerald home battery cabinet at the side and an emerald electric sedan in the driveway. A fine green architectural LED line follows the facade. Architectural photography, house dominant, actual lived-in real-world scale, no model base.
