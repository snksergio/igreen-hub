# Visuais V2

Gerados com a ferramenta imagegen integrada, sem CLI. As versões originais permanecem intactas.

## Conexão
Original: `visuals/connection-v2.png`. Imagem usada no site: `dist/assets/connection-v2.webp`.

Use case: ads-marketing. Wide 16:9 luxury electric mobility launch website background, photoreal cinematic studio photography. A deep emerald green elegant electric sports sedan in complete side three-quarter view facing right, parked and physically connected by a graceful luminous pale green charging cable to a refined emerald EV charging pedestal at the right. Both car and charger together in ONE coherent composition, matching perspective and light. Objects occupy lower half from x25% to90%, leave upper half and upper left clean near-black negative space for website text. Black seamless studio, subtle smoked emerald atmosphere, restrained bright green rim lighting, realistic metallic paint and rubber, soft floor reflection. Clearly legible beautiful car silhouette and charger, premium quiet drama. No words, no text, no logos, no people, no UI.

## Dark
Original: `visuals/dark-v2.png`. Imagem usada no site: `dist/assets/dark-v2.webp`.

Use case: ads-marketing. Wide 16:9 ultra-dark luxury automotive teaser photography for electric mobility launch. ONE composition: complete electric coupe side profile facing right, left and center of lower half, and slim charging pedestal at right, a charging cable gracefully linking them. Almost everything is pure black. Car only revealed by a razor-thin soft white-silver rim of light along roof and hood, faint emerald highlights, a restrained red rear light at left and pale headlight at right. Charger is almost invisible except narrow emerald vertical rim and very subtle screen. Objects entirely within frame, occupy lower central half with ample black negative space above for heading and countdown. Like a mysterious premium vehicle unveiling campaign, elegant photographic silhouette, matte black background and floor, no fog clouds, no neon flood, no words no logos no watermark no UI.

## Páginas
- `dist/painel-v2.html`: arco semicircular, guia projetada a partir das coordenadas da imagem, pulsos de leitura da pista e contador com fundo de contraste.
- `dist/revelacao-v2.html`: carro e carregador juntos.
- `dist/revelacao-dark-v2.html`: composição de silhuetas e luzes de contorno.

Todos preservam o lançamento em 01/11/2026 às 20h de Brasília. Pausa das animações e preferência por movimento reduzido continuam disponíveis. Os arquivos CSS e JS da V2 são independentes dos originais.
