# Direção fotográfica — iGreen Voyage

As cinco imagens foram geradas com a ferramenta integrada **ImageGen**, sem CLI/API externa. O usuário aprovou esta direção visual durante a revisão. São imagens conceituais, não fotografias do navio ou do evento reais. Os JPEGs usados no site preservam a composição dos PNGs gerados, com compressão para carregamento mais rápido.

## Arquivos finais

- `public/images/voyage-hero.jpg`: abertura, cruzeiro ao sol.
- `public/images/voyage-pool.jpg`: piscina e solário.
- `public/images/voyage-cabin.jpg`: cabine com varanda.
- `public/images/voyage-dining.jpg`: gastronomia ao pôr do sol.
- `public/images/voyage-night.jpg`: celebração no convés.

Os PNGs de origem foram preservados em `public/images/`, com os mesmos nomes. O site usa apenas os JPEGs.

## Prompts finais

### Abertura

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: cinematic full-bleed website hero background for iGreen Voyage cruise holiday reward experience. Create one photorealistic luxury travel editorial photograph, very wide landscape 16:9 composition. A majestic modern white cruise liner seen from a low helicopter in a three-quarter front view occupies the RIGHT HALF of the image, sailing through luminous clear turquoise and deep cobalt ocean with a natural curling white wake. Distant lush tropical islands at far right horizon. LEFT HALF is mostly open ocean with beautiful sun reflections and subtle waves, ample uninterrupted negative space for typography that will be overlaid in HTML. Horizon at upper third. Warm late afternoon sunshine, golden highlights, inviting vacation feeling, vivid but tasteful true-to-life color, premium tourism campaign, shot on full-frame cinema camera, physically accurate water, crisp detailed white ship with many real balcony decks, blue pools and tiny people aboard. Authentic photographic texture, atmospheric distance. No text, no logos, no illustration, no CGI look, no wireframe, no science fiction, no dark green filter. Output a high-resolution landscape image.

### Piscina

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial background for a luxury cruise vacation website, cinematic photograph, wide 16:9 landscape. Eye-level from a teak sun deck aboard a large white luxury cruise ship, looking across a beautiful azure swimming pool toward an open bright turquoise ocean. White upholstered lounge chairs, striped yellow and cream parasols, white curved ship decks frame right side. A few naturally candid adult vacationers small in the middle distance, relaxing and enjoying a drink, not posing, no clear close-up faces. Sunlight sparkling on pool water, gently windblown linen, warm Mediterranean summer midday with bright blue sky. Foreground pool water beautifully detailed. Travel magazine photography shot with a real camera, honest natural textures, feeling of well-earned holiday, happy relaxed warm premium. No text, no logos, no watermark, no CGI plastic look. Clear spatially coherent ship architecture. Wide landscape high resolution.

### Cabine

Photorealistic luxury travel editorial photography, wide 16:9 image of a beautiful cruise ship balcony stateroom in warm morning sunlight. Cream linen king bed in left foreground, teak details, sheer curtains blowing by open glass balcony doors on right, two comfortable balcony chairs and uninterrupted brilliant blue ocean beyond. Calm airy inviting vacation, realistic intimate spatial proportions of a cruise cabin, real camera natural textured fabrics, sunshine and warm pale yellow accents, no people, no text or logos, no CGI appearance. Concept visual for a cruise reward campaign.

### Gastronomia

Photorealistic high-end travel editorial photograph wide 16:9. An intimate outdoor restaurant table on a luxury cruise ship, golden-hour ocean horizon and white ship railing behind. Two beautifully plated Mediterranean seafood dishes, fresh colorful vegetables, sparkling water glasses and bread on a cream linen tablecloth, elegant realistic details, warm sunshine, candid feeling of a holiday lunch at sea. Softly blurred vacationers on background tables. Luminous cobalt sea and honey sunlight, photographed with full frame camera, authentic photographic textures, not a 3D render, no text no logo. Concept image for cruise reward experience.

### Celebração

Photorealistic cinematic travel editorial photograph, wide 16:9 landscape. Joyful adults celebrating at an elegant open-air party on the pool deck of a cruise ship at blue hour. Warm festoon lights overhead, a few tasteful warm gold confetti pieces suspended in the air, friends in smart casual vacation clothes smiling and dancing naturally in middle ground, softly lit white cruise architecture at right, deep blue sea and pink sunset horizon visible left, tropical holiday reward celebration. Premium candid event photography with authentic natural people, warm skin tones, gentle motion blur, no text no logos no plastic CGI appearance.
